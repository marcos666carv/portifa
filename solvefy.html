Solvefy

A communications company two decades in the making, reborn as a technology ecosystem. I owned design and growth through the change.

I
Chapter I

The rebrand

Solvefy came from Ativos Capital, a company built over 20 years. I coordinated the rebrand that took 5 disconnected brands with inconsistent messaging and turned them into one integrated business ecosystem.

01Research first. Stakeholder interviews and market analysis grounded the rebrand, and we defined the metrics we would track to prove it worked.
02One ecosystem. Brand, communication tracks and social strategy unified across every sub brand, plus two live events.
03A system, not just a logo. A design system shipped alongside the brand, orchestrating every product and touchpoint under one language.
04Built for sales too. Sales playbooks and events gave the commercial team a clear path to sell the new ecosystem.
5 brands to 1 ecosystem

Chapter II

The product

I reorganized how the product portfolio was structured across four products. NPS climbed, and navigation and campaign dispatch got noticeably smoother for every team using it.

01Four products, one direction. Disparo Pro, Brasilfone, EasyCDP and Solvefy Cloud, working close to tech and operations.
02End to end craft. Information architecture, wireframes, high fidelity prototypes and responsive systems built to scale, not one offs.
03NPS climbed. Navigation and campaign dispatch got noticeably smoother for every team using the products daily.

Chapter III

The AI

I shipped AI driven design workflows while the company launched Agents, its AI orchestration product. Design that ships with AI, not around it.

01AI in the process. Design workflows built and shipped on AI, run through Claude Code, MCPs and custom agents, shortening delivery cycles for the whole team.
02AI in the product too. While I worked on this, Solvefy launched Agents, its own AI orchestration product for customers.
03Not on the slide. The distinction that matters: AI as something I build with daily, not a line in a deck.

02Winning by Design. Seeded as the team's operating culture, not a one off workshop.
03Knowledge that travels. Stronger knowledge sharing so the team's work compounded instead of restarting every time.
