04Three strengths — and how each one unfolds through a project

01

I find the product's real object.

Under every B2B interface there is one thing the business actually runs on. At Leads2b it was the cadence, not the screens. At Remessa Online it was the one question every visitor arrives with: how much do I actually receive?

How it unfolds in a project
1Research first. Interviews, churn data and support tickets until the real object surfaces — not the assumed one.
2Rebuild the architecture around it. Information architecture, flows and naming reorganize around the object, not around org charts.
3Name the constraints out loud. Remessa went eight simulators to three, not one — the limit was technical, and we said so.
4The structure outlives the designer. Anyone can open Leads2b today and see the division still shipping, four years on.
↳8 → 3simulators consolidated
↳4 yearsthe structure still guides Leads2b
↳Churnstabilized while it settled in
02

Design and growth in one seat.

I do not hand the business problem to someone else after the mockups. At Solvefy and Remessa Online I ran design and growth together, so every design decision had a revenue question attached.

How it unfolds in a project
1Ground decisions in pain, not opinion. I brought churn research into how Solvefy designed and shipped.
2Design where the money moves. Portfolio reorganization, navigation and campaign dispatch — the flows customers pay through.
3Arm the commercial team. Sales playbooks and two live events gave sales a clear path to sell the new ecosystem.
4Measure in business terms. Clients retained, revenue kept, NPS moved — not screens delivered.
↳18%of revenue kept
↳3large clients brought back
↳NPS ↑after the portfolio reorganization
03

AI in the process and in the product.

Claude Code, MCPs and custom agents in my daily workflow since July 2025. Not a slide about AI — shipped workflows the whole team ran on, and a product I designed and built end to end myself.

How it unfolds in a project
1Start with the team's bottleneck. AI driven design workflows shortened delivery cycles for the whole design team at Solvefy.
2Then build product with it. An internal leads engine, designed and built end to end — workspaces per ICP, in the sales team's hands.
3Tie it to an OKR, not a demo. The engine had a monthly target of 200 + 100 qualified leads. Its first day of use cleared the month's target.
4Ship alongside the company's AI bet. All of it while Solvefy launched Agents, its AI orchestration product.
↳Day 1monthly lead target beaten
↳200 + 100leads per month, the bar it cleared
↳Since 2025Claude Code, MCPs, agents daily
